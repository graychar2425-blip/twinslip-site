Pro pays for itself the first time you bill a client.

Free covers one agent and one bank or card account, with every purchase and all your history. Pro covers every agent and every account. If your agents' costs are your clients' costs, Pro also turns them into a statement you can send: every line with how it was checked, your handling fee as its own line, and the lines marked billed with your invoice number. It keeps watch over your agents' spending and adds what an accountant or a tax form needs.

Your ledger lives on your computer. We never see it. You pay us to keep the watch steady and the tools sharp: checks that flag what needs you, tax forms current every year, and parsers maintained as banks and platforms change underneath you.

If you cancel

Cancelling Pro never takes your data or the basic tracker. The Pro tools switch off when the time you paid for runs out. Everything you recorded stays on your computer and everything already counted stays counted. From then on, purchases from agents beyond the free one wait in your inbox again, saved in full, and the free tracker and plain export keep working.

Do I need an account?

No Twinslip account. Lemon Squeezy emails you a Pro key when you buy, and you paste it into the app once.

Does Pro need the internet?

Once, to switch it on. After that every Pro tool works offline. The app checks your key again about once a month, and if it can't get through it keeps working for another 30 days.

What does Free cover?

One agent and one bank or card account, with every purchase, all your history and plain export. The agent is the first one that reports, unless you choose another in Settings. Purchases from other agents are never dropped: they're saved in your inbox, marked, and count for their agent once you have Pro. A second account's statement is kept and listed in full; with Pro it checks what your agents report too. The iPhone and receipts you attach aren't accounts.

Monthly or yearly?

Either: $10 a month or $96 a year per person. Anyone who buys by 15 April 2027 pays $72 a year for as long as they stay subscribed.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes: each seat covers one person on up to two of their computers. To move to a new one, open Menu → Settings → Pro on the old computer and click "Remove Pro from this computer", then paste your key on the new one.
