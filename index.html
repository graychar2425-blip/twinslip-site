What it does

One ledger for every purchase your agents make, from the moment they pay to the invoice your client receives. The screens are the real app with demo data.

Capture automatically

Agents report each purchase as they make it: subscriptions, API credits and pay-per-use calls, with receipts from x402 and other payment rails kept as evidence. Card, prepaid credits or crypto. Nobody types anything in.

Free

Verify against your cards

Drop in the statement file your bank or card issuer offers, or import your Stripe Link wallet. When a statement line matches an agent's report, the purchase is marked verified and counted once. A charge no agent reported waits for one tap.

Control spend

Give each agent a monthly budget and a mandate: the merchants it may buy from and the most it may spend at once. Purchases outside those are flagged, and so is a service you're paying for twice. With Pro, scheduled checks and alerts for unusual spending reach you without opening the app.

Budgets and mandates free. Alerts and scheduled checks with Pro

Bill clients

Each client gets a statement as a PDF and a spreadsheet, every line with its evidence and your handling fee as its own line, plus invoice lines to import into your invoicing app. Mark lines billed with your invoice number so nothing is billed twice.

Who it's for

Anyone whose agents spend money that has to be accounted for.

Agencies and consultants

Your agents do client work, and their AI costs belong on the client's invoice. Twinslip keeps what each job cost, adds your handling fee, and gives you a statement with the evidence for every line, so the bill holds up when a client asks.

Developers and teams running agents

Your agents buy API credits, subscriptions and pay-per-use calls on your cards. Twinslip puts every purchase in one ledger, checks it against your statements, and flags an agent that buys outside its budget or mandate. Pro is priced per person; with two seats or more it adds an audit trail.

How it works

Set it up once. After that your agents do the reporting.

  1. Install and connect

    Download the Mac app and double-click to set it up. It connects the agent apps it finds. Then tell your agent "Set up Twinslip." and it does the rest.

  2. Agents report as they pay

    Each purchase lands in the ledger with the amount, merchant, date, job and client. Agents can check their own budget before a sizeable purchase.

  3. Verify, bill and export

    Add your bank or card statement file to verify what agents reported. Then send client statements, hand over the tax pack, or export a spreadsheet.

  • On your Mac

    The ledger is a file on your own computer. No Twinslip server holds a copy.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Free covers one agent and one bank or card account, with every purchase, all your history and plain export. Pro is $10 a month or $96 a year per person, with 3 agents and every account; each extra agent is $3 a month or $29 a year. It adds client billing, alerts, the tax pack and accounting exports. Pro opens in February 2027; anyone who buys by 15 April 2027 pays $72 a year for as long as they stay subscribed. See Pro.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is one person, not an agent or a device, on up to two of their computers. Each seat comes with 3 agents. With two seats or more, Pro also keeps an audit trail.

Which agent apps does it work with?

Claude Code, verified end to end. Setup also connects Claude Desktop, Cursor, Codex and the ChatGPT desktop app, VS Code, Windsurf and Devin Desktop, Gemini CLI and Zed when it finds them; those are connected but not yet verified end to end. Other agents and scripts can report a purchase with one command (untested so far). The page for agents and developers has the details.

Where is my data kept?

In a file on your own computer. There's no Twinslip account and no Twinslip server holding a copy. It stores amounts, merchants and dates, never card numbers, passwords or API keys. Your agent's AI provider does see what your agent reads, like the totals it asks for; the privacy page lists everything that leaves.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. It never logs into your bank and uses no bank-connection service like Plaid. To verify purchases, download the statement file (CSV) your bank or card issuer offers and drop it in. Without it, your agents' own reports and your receipts still work.

Could an agent hide what it spent?

No. Agents can add and read. They can't confirm, delete or hide anything: what they add either counts as their own spending or waits for your tap. What an agent can do is fail to report: a payment made outside your bank (prepaid credits, crypto, another card) stays unseen unless another source catches it. Card payments show up when you import your statement.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

You keep what you paid for. Pro keeps working in every version of Twinslip released before your subscription ends; newer versions run as Free until you renew, and your data is never touched. Everything already counted stays counted; in a version that runs as Free, purchases from agents beyond the free one wait in your inbox, saved in full.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You download a folder, double-click a file, and tell your agent "Set up Twinslip." Your agent does the rest. The download page walks through every click.

Does it work on Windows or iPhone?

The beta download is for Mac. An iPhone app is on the way; it will pass Wallet (Apple Pay) taps and bank texts to the Mac rather than keep a ledger of its own.
