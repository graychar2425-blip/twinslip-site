Download Twinslip

Free, for Mac. Setup takes about ten minutes and you don't need to know how to code. Every click is written out below.

Mac

The beta: a folder with the app and a file you double-click to start it.

Download for Mac

Twinslip-beta-0.1.0.zip, a small zip file. Needs macOS and a free program called Node, which the app helps you install.

It connects the agent apps it finds on your Mac. For Cowork, or to add Twinslip from inside Claude, see other ways to install.

iPhone

Sends Apple Pay (Wallet) taps and the bank texts you set up from your phone to Twinslip on your Mac, through your iCloud Drive. Cards outside Wallet and online purchases aren't captured.

Set it up on your Mac

Have your agent app installed first. Claude Code is checked end to end; the other apps are set up the same way but not yet checked end to end.

  1. Open the download

    Double-click Twinslip-beta-0.1.0.zip in your Downloads folder. You get a folder called Twinslip.

  2. Move the folder to your Home folder

    In Finder, choose Go → Home from the menu bar, then drag the Twinslip folder into that window. Leave it there afterwards; if you move it later, you'll need to do the setup again.

  3. Double-click "Start Twinslip"

    It's inside the folder. The first time, your Mac stops it with a message that it can't check the app, because this beta isn't signed by Apple yet. That's expected. Click Done, not "Move to Trash". Then:

    • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    • Scroll down to the message about "Start Twinslip" and click Open Anyway.
    • Enter your Mac password if it asks, then click Open.

    On macOS 15 and later, right-clicking the file and choosing Open no longer gets past this message. Use Open Anyway in System Settings. You only do this once; after that a normal double-click works.

  4. Install Node if it asks

    Twinslip runs on Node, a free program many apps use. If you don't have it, a page from nodejs.org opens for you. Download the version marked LTS, install it like any other app, then double-click "Start Twinslip" again.

  5. Hand it to your agent

    Twinslip connects itself to the agent apps it finds on your Mac (Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex and the ChatGPT desktop app, VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf or Devin Desktop, Gemini CLI and Zed) and changes nothing else in them. Quit your agent app and open it again so it picks up the connection, then say: "Set up Twinslip."

    Your agent does the rest and tells you if anything is left for you to do. If your agent app can already read your email, it can sweep it for receipts from AI and developer-tool vendors and for bank alerts, right then; Twinslip never gets your email login, and nothing runs on a schedule.

  6. That's it

    Your ledger opens in your web browser. The address starts with 127.0.0.1, which means your own computer, not a website. A checklist there ticks itself off as things start working, and from now on each purchase your agent reports shows up within a second.

Other ways to install

These add Twinslip's tools to Claude from inside it, so your agent can record what it spends. Your ledger page, where you sort purchases and add your bank's statement file, comes with the Mac download. If you've set that up, you don't need these: it already connects the agent apps it finds on your Mac. These ways are new and not yet checked end to end.

Claude Desktop: double-click

Download for Claude Desktop

Double-click twinslip-0.1.0.mcpb or drag it into the Claude window, then confirm the install. You can also choose Settings → Extensions → Advanced settings → Install Extension… and pick the file. Claude Desktop brings its own copy of Node; we haven't checked yet that it's new enough for Twinslip.

Claude Code: two commands

Paste these into your terminal one at a time, or ask Claude Code to run them. You need Claude Code 2.1.224 or later and Node 22.13 or later.

claude plugin marketplace add https://graychar2425-blip.github.io/twinslip-site/claude/marketplace.json
claude plugin install twinslip@twinslip

Then start a new Claude Code session and say: "Set up Twinslip."

Cowork: upload the plugin

Download twinslip-plugin-0.1.0.zip. In Claude, choose Customize → Plugins → Add → Upload plugin and pick the zip. Twinslip runs while Cowork works on your computer, and needs Node 22.13 or later.

After that

Double-click "Start Twinslip" whenever you want to look at your ledger. You can close the window when you're done; your agent keeps recording purchases either way.

Optional: double-check your agent

A purchase is marked verified when something your agent didn't supply saw the charge too: your bank's statement file, your iPhone or a receipt you attach.

  • Your bank: download the statement file (CSV) your bank's website offers and drop it on the ledger page. Twinslip never logs into your bank.
  • A receipt you have: click Attach receipt on the purchase and pick the file.
  • Receipts in your email: ask your agent, "Sweep my email for agent receipts." This works only if your agent can already read your email, and it looks for AI and developer-tool vendors, not everyday shopping. No email in your agent app? Use your bank's statement file instead. Receipts your agent finds are kept as evidence and catch purchases it forgot, but they don't mark anything verified.

Updating

Download the new zip and replace the old Twinslip folder with it. Your data is kept somewhere else, so nothing is lost. Then do steps 3 to 5 again.

Removing it

  • Delete the Twinslip folder.
  • Delete your data: in Finder choose Go → Go to Folder, type ~/.twinslip, and move that folder to the Trash.
  • Remove "twinslip" from your agent app's connector settings.

Something not working?

Take a screenshot of the window and tell us on GitHub. In the ledger, Menu → Settings → "Show beta report" gives counts only, with no merchants, amounts or names, if they ask for it.
