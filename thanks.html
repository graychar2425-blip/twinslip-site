Thanks. Now switch Pro on.

Your Pro key is in your email from Lemon Squeezy. In Twinslip: Menu → Settings → Pro → paste your key → Activate.

Steps

  1. Find the email

    Look for an email from Lemon Squeezy about your Twinslip Pro order. It has your Pro key: a long line of letters and numbers. Copy it.

  2. Open Twinslip

    Double-click "Start Twinslip" if it isn't open already. Don't have it yet? Download it first.

  3. Paste the key

    Click Menu, then Settings, and find the Pro section. Paste your key and click Activate.

  4. That's it

    The Pro tools appear straight away. Do the same on each computer you bought a seat for.

If something goes wrong

I can't find the email

Give it a few minutes, then check your spam or promotions folder and search your inbox for "Lemon Squeezy". Make sure you're looking in the inbox of the email address you typed at checkout.

It says every seat on this key is in use

Each seat covers one person, on up to two of their computers. To move a seat, open Twinslip on the computer you no longer use, go to Menu → Settings → Pro and click "Remove Pro from this computer". Then paste the key on the new computer. If you need Pro on more computers at once, buy more seats.

It couldn't reach the checkout service

Switching Pro on needs the internet once. Connect and click Activate again. After that Pro works offline: the app re-checks your key about once a month and keeps going for 30 more days if it can't get through.

It says it didn't recognise the key

Copy the key again from the email and make sure no spaces or extra characters came along. If you bought more than once, check you're using the key from the right email.

What if I cancel later?

The Pro tools switch off. Your data and the free tracker stay exactly as they are.
